## Source-dependence notes

- S07 is a rendering of S06/Rosstat data, not independent confirmation.
- S27 reproduces/attributes Minfin data and is not independent of S26.
- S19 summarizes S18; use the review as the deeper evidence owner.
- S34 reports Rosstat data; it is not independent of Rosstat underlying statistics.
- CBR forecasts/assessments are authoritative for the Bank of Russia's view, not proof of future outcomes.
- Post-Q2 sources S11/S12/S21/S23/S29/S31 are allowed to interpret Q2 or provide subsequently released Q2 data, but their Q3 observations are labeled as monitoring/outlook rather than retroactively treated as Q2 facts.
