# Project Decisions — WORK-0001

These are project-local decisions authorized by the Commission. They do not own external economic facts.

## DEC-001 — Reader language

**Decision:** the maintained standalone Knowledge Product is written in Russian.

**Basis:** the source Commission and HUMAN INBOX are Russian; the named consumer is a professional Russian banking user. This is a publication/consumer decision, not a scientific claim.

## DEC-002 — One Scientific owner and reader edition

**Decision:** `_mw/knowledge/RUSSIAN-ECONOMY-Q2-2026.md` is both the maintained Current Scientific Knowledge owner and the standalone reader Product for this knowledge-only contour.

**Reason:** a separate editable final-report artifact would duplicate current scientific truth without a distinct lifecycle or Authority need. Research History and verification remain separate owners.

## DEC-003 — No standalone Theme Map / Thematic Synthesis layer

**Decision:** after exhaustive `RC-0001` census, the six transparent, bounded Research Results were reconciled directly through corpus-level `RS-0001` rather than creating separate Theme Map and per-theme synthesis artifacts.

**Reason:** the corpus is small enough for exact accounting; the material mechanisms are strongly cross-cutting. A separate theme hierarchy would add process surface without improving coverage, independence or reader value. MADARAII-11 explicitly permits smaller corpora to avoid a separate map when it adds little value.

## DEC-004 — Research cutoff and late-input handling

**Decision:** freeze the substantive Research universe at 2026-09-11 before the scheduled 11 September Rosstat first Q2 GDP estimate and Bank of Russia 13:30 MSK rate decision, then perform one bounded freshness check before closure. Same-day Q2 data are assimilated only if available and material. The detailed Q2 balance-of-payments publication scheduled for 30 September is a post-closure reopen trigger.

**Reason:** the Product concerns end-Q2 rather than live September conditions; indefinite waiting for later vintages would violate the completion/stop requirement.

## DEC-005 — Generic-bank reliance boundary

**Decision:** bank implications are expressed for a generic Russian bank focused on corporate lending and materially exposed to AПК, without institution-specific portfolio calibration.

**Reason:** borrower mix, internal risk metrics, loan terms and portfolio weights were not provided. Inferring them would exceed evidence.
