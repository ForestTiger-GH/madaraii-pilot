# Project Decisions — WORK-0001

These are project-local decisions authorized by the Commission. They do not own external economic facts.

## DEC-001 — Reader language

**Decision:** the maintained standalone Knowledge Product is written in Russian.

**Basis:** the source Commission and HUMAN INBOX are Russian; the named consumer is a professional Russian banking user. This is a publication/consumer decision, not a scientific claim.

## DEC-002 — One Scientific owner and reader edition

**Decision:** `_mw/knowledge/RUSSIAN-ECONOMY-Q2-2026.md` is both the maintained Current Scientific Knowledge owner and the standalone reader Product for this knowledge-only contour.

**Reason:** a separate editable final-report artifact would duplicate current scientific truth without a distinct lifecycle or Authority need. Research History and verification remain separate owners.

## DEC-003 — No standalone Theme Map / Thematic Synthesis layer

**Decision:** after exhaustive `RC-0001` census, the seven transparent, bounded Research Results are reconciled directly through corpus-level `RS-0001` rather than creating separate Theme Map and per-theme synthesis artifacts.

**Reason:** the corpus is small enough for exact accounting; the material mechanisms are strongly cross-cutting. A separate theme hierarchy would add process surface without improving coverage, independence or reader value. Reopen only if corpus geometry expands materially.

## DEC-004 — Research cutoff and late-input handling

**Decision:** freeze the substantive Research universe at 2026-09-11 before later same-day macro publications. Material later data enter only through explicit revalidation/rebinding; detailed Q2 balance-of-payments data and later GDP vintages are reopen triggers rather than silent replacements.

**Reason:** the Product concerns end-Q2 and requires a reproducible evidence vintage. Indefinite rolling updates would prevent truthful closure.

## DEC-005 — Generic-bank reliance boundary

**Decision:** bank implications are expressed for a generic Russian bank focused on corporate lending and materially exposed to AПК, without institution-specific portfolio calibration.

**Reason:** borrower mix, internal risk metrics, loan terms and portfolio weights were not provided. Inferring them would exceed evidence.

## DEC-006 — Supplemental IFRS evidence lane

**Decision:** `INBOX-0002` materially expands the evidence universe and therefore activates `TOPIC-07` / `RR-07` before Research fan-in and Scientific assembly.

**Reason:** the user explicitly required IFRS reporting of major Russian banks as an additional source for economic trends, phenomena and effects. The original six-topic fan-in became stale until this lane was added.

## DEC-007 — IFRS comparability rule

**Decision:** IFRS disclosures of Sber, VTB, Gazprombank, Russian Agricultural Bank, Moscow Credit Bank and Sovcombank are used as institution-specific observation lanes and are never mechanically aggregated into national statistics.

**Reason:** portfolio mix, bank strategy, IFRS 9 models, consolidation perimeter, state role and risk appetite materially affect reported loan growth and risk metrics.

## DEC-008 — No Target WHAT / Target HOW layer

**Decision:** no separate Target WHAT, Target HOW or Engineering Product realization work is activated in this contour.

**Reason:** the commissioned Product is maintained analytical knowledge itself, not a target bank policy, software system or operating mechanism. Human outcome requirements remain owned by the developed input and Work contract.

## DEC-009 — Verification reliance boundary

**Decision:** `VR-0001` verifies the final Science for strategic/professional informational use. It does not support borrower underwriting, bank-specific portfolio forecasts, regulatory/accounting conclusions or quantitative causal elasticities.

**Reason:** the source universe lacks commissioning-bank internal data and borrower-level cash-flow/debt schedules; verification independence is also limited to a logically separate pass by the same agent/session.

## DEC-010 — Assurance composition

**Decision:** source/Result census is exhaustive; claim correctness is checked through a risk-based high-consequence sample plus causal/definition/temporal coverage audits.

**Reason:** exhaustive bibliography accounting and every-sentence substantive verification are different assurance claims. `VR-0001` preserves that distinction.

All decisions are terminal/current within `WORK-0001` unless their stated basis changes under a new Commission or explicit delta.
