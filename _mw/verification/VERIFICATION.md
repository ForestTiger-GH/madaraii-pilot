## 4. Causal and interpretive verification

### 4.1 GDP versus corporate condition

The document does not infer borrower health from GDP growth. It explicitly reconciles positive Q2 GDP with falling H1 investment and corporate profit. This is supported by independent official data families and is analytically valid. **PASS.**

### 4.2 Rate transmission

The mechanism “lower key rate → lower cash interest for floating-rate debt” is directly supported by Bank of Russia evidence on floating-rate prevalence. The document does not quantify an economy-wide ICR benefit and explicitly limits borrower-level magnitude. **PASS.**

### 4.3 Investment decline

The document treats high rates as one material mechanism rather than the uniquely identified cause of the -9.9% H1 investment change. Demand, project-cycle and other factors remain possible. **PASS.**

### 4.4 Corporate credit acceleration

The candidate rejects the unsupported interpretation “credit growth = capex revival” and reconciles credit with working capital, bridge/refinancing, budget timing, projects, subsidies and bank supply strategy. Direct support exists for fiscal timing and bank strategy; exact decomposition remains UNKNOWN. **PASS.**

### 4.5 Credit-risk maturation

The phrase “early and heterogeneous materialization of corporate credit risk” is supported by the combination of risky restructurings/system data and Stage/NPL/CoR movement across several large banks. The candidate does not claim an economy-wide default cycle or arithmetic cross-bank aggregation. **PASS.**

### 4.6 Fiscal mechanism

The document distinguishes cash deficit/front-loading from structural fiscal impulse. It uses budget timing primarily to explain recipient liquidity and credit demand, where Bank of Russia and GPB evidence is directionally supportive. **PASS.**

### 4.7 FX mechanism

The ruble mechanism is framed as conditional on net FX exposure and does not infer uniform exporter/importer effects. **PASS.**

### 4.8 AПК

The candidate treats H1 profit, stocks, producer prices, fuel, seasonality and preferential credit as interacting factors and explicitly separates current credit quality from post-harvest repayment capacity. It avoids treating crop volume or current NPL as sufficient. **PASS.**

No causal statement requiring repair was identified.
