## Provocation trace

### P01 — The underwriter steals the steering wheel

I treated the borrower typology in §9 as a machine-readable segmentation table. It is not one taxonomy: rows mix source of demand (`consumer-facing`), investment position, contract form (`state contractor`), FX/business model (`exporter`), leverage (`high-leverage`), size (`SME`) and sector/cash-cycle (`АПК`). One borrower can occupy several rows simultaneously. The sentence after the table correctly says it is a macro overlay rather than automatic credit-quality classification, but the visual form still resembles a partition.

**Observation:** useful archetypes can be misread as an ontology. This is a representation/misuse vulnerability, not an underwriting defect claim.

### P02 — The standalone Product calls itself a candidate

Opening only the reader Product shows `candidate Current Scientific Knowledge / standalone knowledge product; итоговая пригодность определяется VR-0001`. `SCI-ADMISSION-0001` later admits this exact blob and intentionally leaves the production header unchanged.

**Observation:** lifecycle ownership is internally coherent, but the standalone file's first screen looks less current than the lifecycle owner says it is. This is an affordance surprise, not a competing-truth finding.

### P03 — The floating-rate fairy

The final Product repeatedly says the large floating-rate share means falling key rates already reduce cash interest for a large part of borrowers. The underlying `RR-02` is more qualified: actual expense depends on debt structure, repricing date, floating/fixed formula and subsidies.

**Observation:** the Product preserves the mechanism but compresses a material repricing-lag qualification where the executive claim is strongest. Direction remains supported; no magnitude error is established.

### P04 — Consumer diesel becomes an agricultural cost shock

The АПК section uses S09 to frame a June fuel shock. S09 is explicitly a Rosstat **consumer-price** series for gasoline and diesel. Fuel is obviously material to seasonal field/logistics costs, but the exact transport from retail consumer prices to farm procurement prices, bulk contracts or realized agricultural fuel expense is not established by this evidence lane.

**Observation:** the direction is plausible, while the target-specific magnitude is not proved by the cited measurand.

### P05 — Grain is crowned king of all АПК

The strongest H1/Q2 empirical АПК lane is crop-heavy: grain stocks, wheat/corn producer prices, harvest/realization, export policy, fuel and seasonal crop lending. H1 financial-result data aggregate crop + livestock. Livestock and processing receive useful conceptual submodels but materially thinner direct Q2 evidence.

**Observation:** the Product itself says crop, livestock and processing require different models; therefore the broad label `АПК` is less evidentially uniform than the section title can suggest. This may be proportionate, but the evidence geometry is asymmetric.

### P06 — Six banks in one trench coat

I forced the IFRS lane to pretend it represented the Russian corporate sector. It failed safely in the deeper Research: `RR-07` explicitly says the six-bank set is purpose-bound and non-statistical; source notes mark Sber and VTB secondary renderings and dependent lineages. The final Product also warns against arithmetic aggregation.

**Observation:** the compressed phrase that major-bank IFRS “confirms” early heterogeneous risk materialization is defensible as an existence/heterogeneity signal but can be overread as prevalence evidence. The stronger anti-pattern of statistical aggregation was successfully avoided.

### P07 — Ask the generic bank for its actual balance sheet

I demanded an operational bank decision from every paragraph headed `Что это означает для корпоративного банка` and searched for portfolio weights, maturity structure, pricing policy, capital constraints, internal rating mix and concentration data.

**Observation:** those data are intentionally absent. The Product is a generic strategic macro overlay, yet its rhetoric sometimes approaches action language (`для банка важно`, `основной риск`, `правильный вопрос`). Directional reasoning can be mistaken for bank-specific portfolio guidance unless the reliance boundary is preserved. `INPUT-0001`, DEC-005, KPA and the Product itself all disclose the missing calibration, so the hidden dependency is disciplined consumer use rather than undisclosed ignorance.

### P08 — Force the archetypes into one classification geometry

The Product first argues that source of final demand may be more informative than broad industry, then later presents archetypes spanning demand source, leverage, size, sector, contract model and FX exposure.

**Observation:** reality probably requires several simultaneous lenses, but the later table does not explicitly say its rows are non-exclusive dimensions. A literal reader or downstream AI can infer a false ontology from a useful heuristic list.

### P09 — Turn a reasoning aid into the One True Necklace

The original human carrier explicitly says the transmission chain `environment → firm → corporate finance → borrower state → credit → bank` is a reasoning aid and must not become a mechanical formula. `INPUT-0001` preserves that constraint. Subject §4.3 then states `Поэтому корректный macro overlay строится так:` and presents a linear sequence `demand/volume → price → costs → margin → working capital → cash flow → capex → cash interest → principal`.

**Observation:** the sequence is useful, but the wording hardens an aid into something closer to canonical order and visually suppresses feedback. Credit terms alter working capital/capex; restructurings alter future cash flow; bank supply is not merely an endpoint. Later sections recover some of these interactions, so this is semantic hardening rather than proof that the whole Product is one-way.

### P10 — Let the five forces become astrology

I treated the heading `Пять сил` as a complete ontology and asked why there are exactly five. Later material mechanisms include bank-supply strategy, balance-sheet starting position, program eligibility, biological seasonality and credit-quality recognition. These can be nested into the five headings, but the number five is a narrative compression rather than a demonstrated complete decomposition.

**Observation:** harmless for an essay; easy for a method-hungry reader to overfit into a reusable framework it was never proven to be.

### P11 — Confidence labels put on a lab coat

I tried to interpret `Высокая уверенность` and `Средняя уверенность` as calibrated probabilities and searched for thresholds, scoring or source-count rules.

**Observation:** the attack mostly failed. No quantitative calibration is claimed; the labels function as qualitative reader aids and are paired with an explicit UNKNOWN list. A downstream summarizer could still inflate them into pseudo-probabilities, but the Product itself does not.
