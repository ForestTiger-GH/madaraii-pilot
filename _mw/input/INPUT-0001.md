## Recovered information need

The requested knowledge is decision-support context for professional bank users who need to understand how the Russian economic regime at end-Q2 2026 changes the economics of corporate borrowers and therefore the environment for corporate lending, with a dedicated agribusiness transmission model where sector-specific mechanisms alter the general corporate picture.
