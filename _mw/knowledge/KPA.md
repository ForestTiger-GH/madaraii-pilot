## Purpose and consumer jobs

The maintained Knowledge Product must let a competent Russian banking reader, without reading raw Research History:

- understand the economic regime at end-Q2 2026 and how H1/2025 produced it;
- translate macro mechanisms into borrower revenue, margins, cash generation, working-capital need, capex, debt service and financing demand;
- distinguish borrower groups for which the same macro impulse has different effects;
- interpret corporate-credit growth and quality without equating loan growth with economic strength;
- use IFRS reporting of major Russian banks as an additional, institution-bounded observation lane on borrower behavior and risk recognition;
- understand AПК as a distinct seasonal/support/commodity-price transmission system;
- distinguish observed facts from interpretation, causal explanation, forecast and uncertainty;
- identify material forward indicators and reopen conditions.

Permitted reliance: strategic/analytical context for bank leadership, strategy and corporate business. It is not an institution-specific underwriting model, portfolio forecast, regulatory opinion or accounting statement.

## Knowledge Assembly Contract — KAC-0001

**Subject:** Russian economic environment at end-Q2 2026 as it matters for corporate borrowers, corporate lending and material AПК exposure.

**Baseline/source universe:** exhaustive `RC-0001`: `RR-01…RR-07`, source registry S01–S53, and cross-topic reconciliation `RS-0001`.

**Temporal boundary:** primary Q2 2026; H1 2026 immediate context; 2025 comparative/causal base; earlier history only when already required by admitted Research.

**Materiality:** retain a fact/mechanism only if it changes understanding of borrower cash economics, debt-service capacity, financing demand, credit selection/quality, sector heterogeneity, or AПК transmission; contextual macro data without such a role are amputated from the ordinary Product.

**Semantic-role coverage:** for each material concern preserve, as applicable: observed evidence, mechanism/causal explanation, alternative account, limits, heterogeneity/applicability, corporate-finance transmission, bank relevance, uncertainty and forward indicator. Roles need not become visible headings.

**IFRS evidence requirement:** major-bank H1/Q2 2026 IFRS reporting is a mandatory supplementary evidence lane. It may corroborate, differentiate or challenge system/macroeconomic conclusions, but bank-level balances, stages and cost-of-risk metrics retain institution-specific definitions and applicability. No mechanical cross-bank aggregation is permitted.

**Emergent discovery:** allowed inside the admitted frozen corpus. New external Research is outside assembly unless verification identifies a material unsupported gap and Work State explicitly reopens Research.

**Late input:** publications after the source cutoff do not enter silently. Material later vintages require an explicit Scientific/Research delta and rebinding. Detailed Q2 balance-of-payments data scheduled after the cutoff and later GDP vintages remain reopen triggers rather than implicit replacements.

**Assurance depth:** exhaustive source/Result accounting; full coverage check against the ten reader-answer obligations from `INPUT-0001` and the IFRS requirement from `INPUT-0002`; risk-based trace of all headline numerical claims and all causal claims with high bank consequence; explicit definition checks for credit-quality measures; dedicated AПК depth check; temporal-vintage audit.

**Owner/admission:** `WORK-0001` Commission authorizes project-local Scientific Knowledge admission after assembly and verification. Verification may determine fitness but does not manufacture missing evidence.

**Read path:** `_mw/WORKSPACE.md` → final Scientific Knowledge owner for ordinary use. Research History only for deep challenge or update.

**Lookup path:** source IDs in the final document → `_mw/evidence/SOURCES.md` → original locator; named mechanism/section → relevant RR/RS only when deeper reasoning is required.

**Publication:** one Markdown maintained edition at the current owner path. No editable duplicate is required.

**Completeness:** bounded to the declared consumer job, timeframe, generic-bank profile and admitted source universe; it does not claim complete knowledge of the Russian economy or any bank-specific portfolio.

**Research Amputation criterion:** a competent bank reader can answer the ten Commission questions, understand the additional IFRS evidence contribution and assess evidence/limits from the final owner without opening `RR-01…RR-07`. Raw Results remain optional for challenge, not mandatory for ordinary understanding.

**Closure:** all source units have a disposition, reader obligations pass verification, materially unresolved items have an owner/reopen trigger, and no target design/implementation obligation is implied.

## Problem-space architecture for assembly

The maintained Knowledge should privilege these connected problem spaces, while allowing the readable document to choose the clearest narrative:

1. **Economic regime:** weak/heterogeneous growth, demand composition, investment, inflation, labor/capacity.
2. **Corporate-finance transmission:** revenue/price-cost spread, profitability, cash conversion, capex, leverage/interest coverage, borrower heterogeneity.
3. **Financial and credit regime:** rate pass-through, new-money economics, supply selection, credit purpose, restructurings/quality.
4. **Offsets and redistributions:** fiscal timing, state demand, exports, ruble/import costs, cross-sector effects.
5. **Bank-observation lane:** major-bank IFRS signals on credit growth, stage migration/NPL, provisioning, corporate liquidity and portfolio strategy, with institution-specific transfer limits.
6. **АПК special case:** biological/seasonal cycle, producer prices, inventory, input costs, harvest realization, preferential finance/support, segment differences.
7. **Forward state:** robust conclusions, uncertainty and monitor/reopen indicators.

This architecture is a knowledge geometry, not a mandatory table of contents.

## Study and lookup paths

**Study path:** executive regime model → interaction of demand/monetary/fiscal/external/cost channels → corporate-finance translation → corporate credit and bank IFRS cross-check → AПК special model → borrower differentiation/bank implications → uncertainty and monitoring.

**Lookup path:** headings and compact tables allow direct access to major borrower archetypes, credit implications, AПК mechanisms, IFRS signals, uncertainty, monitoring indicators and source roles. Core statements cite source IDs. Exact URLs remain resolvable through the source registry and a compact reference section in the standalone publication.

## Claim and evidence contract

- Every headline number names period and metric; source ID follows material numerical claims or the paragraph containing them.
- Source-attributed causality is labeled as source assessment when it is not independently identified.
- Project inference uses bounded language and an explicit limit where magnitude is unmeasured.
- Forecasts name forecaster/date/vintage and are never presented as observed Q2 fact.
- Financial-result data are labeled nominal pre-tax and their excluded organization universe is stated.
- Corporate credit “problem” 11.8% and IV–V 4.2% are never conflated; AПК IV–V 2.1% is compared only to the same narrow metric.
- IFRS Stage 3/POCI, NPL90+, bank NPL and Russian regulatory quality categories are never treated as interchangeable definitions.
- Bank IFRS loan/deposit growth is never presented as a national statistic without system evidence.
- AПК H1 profit is read with seasonality; harvest forecasts are future information, not Q2 realized performance.

## Lifecycle and recovery

Correction/update changes the current Scientific owner and records the evidence delta. Research History remains preserved. A material new Q2 data vintage requires selective revalidation of affected claims rather than automatic full-project restart.

The Q2 document remains historically current for its declared cutoff after closure. Later monitoring should form a new contour or explicit delta rather than silently rewrite the historical evidence boundary.

One KPA artifact is justified because the final knowledge has multiple source roles, consumer jobs, verification criteria and future-vintage triggers. One final Scientific Knowledge file is sufficient for maintained knowledge and reader publication. Separate source registry, Research History and verification evidence remain distinct owners.

Cold-reader test target: from `_mw/WORKSPACE.md`, a reader reaches the final document in one owner resolution and can study it end-to-end; source drill-down requires at most one additional registry route.
