## 3. Which directions proved material and why

### Economic regime and investment

Q2 GDP returned to positive annual growth after a weak Q1, while H1 industrial production was broadly flat and fixed investment fell sharply. This established a regime of **heterogeneous cooling rather than synchronized recovery or recession**. For a corporate bank, the investment decline materially changes project-credit demand and the revenue outlook of investment-facing borrowers.

### Corporate profitability and cash flow

H1 corporate financial results deteriorated substantially more than aggregate output, with large sector dispersion. This made the distinction `output ≠ profit ≠ cash flow ≠ debt service` central to the final model.

### Monetary transmission

The key rate was already declining, and a large share of corporate debt carries floating rates. Existing borrowers therefore began receiving interest-expense relief. New market borrowing remained expensive. This created a two-speed financial regime: easing of legacy debt service alongside a high hurdle rate for new money and refinancing.

### Fiscal and external offsets

Budget timing, state contracts, exports and the ruble materially redistributed demand and liquidity between borrower groups. These channels explained why some firms could preserve cash flow while the private investment cycle weakened.

### Corporate credit and risk recognition

Corporate financing accelerated in Q2 even as fixed investment fell. Research therefore separated investment, working capital, bridge financing, refinancing, support programs, large projects and bank supply strategy. Risk evidence also showed that stress can surface through restructurings, Stage migration and provisioning before a broad NPL spike.

### Major-bank IFRS reporting

IFRS reporting of Sber, VTB, Gazprombank, Russian Agricultural Bank, Moscow Credit Bank and Sovcombank demonstrated large differences in corporate-loan growth and risk recognition. This strengthened two conclusions:

- observed corporate-credit growth reflects bank strategy and portfolio mix as well as borrower demand;
- H1 2026 showed early, uneven maturation of corporate credit risk rather than one synchronized default cycle.

### АПК

АПК materially broke the general corporate model because biological seasonality, inventories, producer prices, fuel/input costs, export realization and preferential lending determine both cash timing and effective credit conditions. H1 weak profitability could therefore coexist with comparatively stable current credit-quality metrics until the post-harvest repayment test.

## 4. How accumulated Research was reconciled

`RC-0001` exhaustively accounted all seven Results and external source identities S01–S53, including shared-source and bank-specific lineages.

A separate theme-map/per-theme synthesis layer was deliberately omitted. Seven Results were directly addressable, while the economically important mechanisms crossed Topic boundaries. `RS-0001` therefore performed direct corpus-level reconciliation.

The synthesis resolved the apparent contradictions:

- **GDP up / profits down:** output and corporate financial result measure different economic layers;
- **credit up / investment down:** debt funds more than capex and also reflects bank supply strategy;
- **broad problem loans up / narrow IV–V stable:** different indicators observe different stages of risk;
- **weak АПК profit / stable specialized-bank NPL:** seasonality, support and portfolio selection shift the timing of credit deterioration;
- **strong exporter profit / weak industrial volume:** prices, FX and physical output can move differently.

The resulting central model became:

> heterogeneous demand and cost environment + still-expensive capital + fiscal/external offsets → divergence in corporate revenue, margins and cash conversion → weaker capex and altered financing needs → selective debt-service stress and risk recognition → selective corporate credit.

## 8. Remaining limitations and material uncertainty

The contour deliberately preserves several bounded UNKNOWNs rather than manufacturing precision:

- no aggregate Q2 EBITDA/OCF/ICR/DSCR universe for Russian companies;
- Rosstat H1 corporate-profit statistics used here exclude SMEs;
- exact decomposition of corporate-credit growth between capex, working capital, bridge/refinancing and stress funding is unavailable;
- borrower-level causes of Stage/NPL migration are often undisclosed;
- exact portfolio and borrower mix of the commissioning bank was not provided;
- later detailed Q2 balance-of-payments data and post-harvest АПК outcomes lie beyond the frozen source universe;
- IFRS observations are institution-specific and not a statistical sample of the corporate sector;
- quantitative causal elasticities were not estimated.

These limitations are explicit in the final Product and owned in `_mw/state/QUESTIONS.md`. None blocks the admitted strategic/analytical reliance.
